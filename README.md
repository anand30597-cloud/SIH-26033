## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.

• Provides logistics support.

• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.

• Lower prices for consumers.

• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
1. Proposed Solution

Solution Name: UzhavarLink – Smart Farm-to-Market Network

UzhavarLink is a digital agricultural marketplace that connects farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), retailers, restaurants and consumers through a single platform.

Our solution introduces a Harvest-to-Order system, where farmers can list their available crops along with quantity, quality, location and expected harvesting date.

How it works:

1.Farmers register and upload their available crops.

2.Buyers can directly view products, compare prices and place orders.

3.The platform combines orders from nearby buyers to reduce transportation costs.

4.A smart demand prediction system estimates which crops will be needed in different locations.

5.A shared logistics system connects farmers with available delivery services.

6.Digital payments and order tracking provide transparency throughout the process.

Innovation and Uniqueness:

*Harvest-to-Order: Buyers can reserve crops before harvesting.

*Group Buying: Multiple consumers can combine their orders to obtain better prices.

*Shared Transport: Nearby farmers can share transportation instead of hiring separate vehicles.

*Price Transparency: Farmers and buyers can compare market prices before making transactions.

*Digital Crop Identity: Every product receives a QR-based record containing its farmer, origin and harvest information.

## Technical Approach

Technologies to be used:

*Frontend: HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

*Backend: Python with Flask or Django.

*Database: MySQL or Firebase.

*Maps and Location: OpenStreetMap.

*Demand Forecasting: Python with basic machine learning.

*Route Optimization: Distance-based route planning algorithms.

*QR Code: Product identification and tracking.

*Payment Integration: Secure digital payment gateway.

Implementation Methodology:

1.Develop separate registration systems for farmers and buyers.

2.Create a marketplace to upload and purchase agricultural products.

3.Implement price comparison and order management.

4.Develop a group-ordering system for nearby consumers.

5.Integrate location-based farmer and buyer matching.

6.Implement demand forecasting using historical sales data.

7.Add shared transportation and delivery tracking.

8.Test the platform with sample farmers, buyers and products.

## Feasibility and Viability
Feasibility:

The proposed solution can be developed using affordable, open-source technologies. It does not require farmers to purchase expensive equipment or install specialized hardware.

A mobile-friendly website can initially serve as the platform, reducing development and maintenance costs.

Potential Challenges:

*Farmers may have limited digital knowledge.

*Agricultural prices may change frequently.

*Transportation availability may be limited in rural areas.

*Product quality may vary between suppliers.

*Farmers and buyers may have concerns about digital transactions.

Strategies to Overcome Challenges:

*Provide a simple interface with regional language support.

*Display regularly updated market prices.

*Partner with local transport providers and FPOs.

*Introduce product quality verification and buyer feedback.

*Use secure payment gateways and digital order records.

*Provide assistance through local farmer groups.

Viability:

The platform can generate revenue through a small transparent service fee on completed transactions and optional logistics services. Basic farmer registration and product listing can remain free.
